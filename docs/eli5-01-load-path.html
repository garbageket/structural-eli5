01 · Foundations of structural behaviour

How loads flow through a building

Every kilonewton you put on a floor has to reach the ground. Follow it down: slab, beam, column, footing, soil.

Each column owns a patch of floor

A column carries the floor area closest to it, called its tributary area. Change the bays, pick a column, and watch the load add up floor after floor.

Why ground-floor columns are huge

The same column at three heights. Load piles up, so the column grows. Slide the floors and watch the size.

Check yourself

Keep in mind

  • 1Quick check on any column: tributary area × floor load × number of floors. Compare it to your model.
  • 2Load goes where the stiffness leads, not where you draw the arrows.
  • 3Check the load at three places: the slab, the column, the footing.
  • 4Edge and corner columns see less gravity but more moment.
