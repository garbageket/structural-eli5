Slabs: one-way, two-way and flat slabs

A slab spans in one direction or two, depending on its shape. A flat slab has no beams, and its danger is a column punching through it.

Which way does a slab span?

Stretch the slab. A square panel splits its load both ways. A long thin one passes almost everything to its short side.

Flat slabs: the column punches through

With no beams, the column pushes up through the slab like a punch through paper. The check is shear around the column, at half the slab depth.

Check yourself

Keep in mind

  • 1If ly / lx > 2, design as one-way. Do not skimp on distribution steel.
  • 2Slab thickness is set by deflection, not strength. Thicker is usually cheaper than more steel.
  • 3Flat slabs: always check punching at the column and at drop panel edges.
  • 4Edge and corner columns punch with unbalanced moment. Their stresses are higher than this simple check.
