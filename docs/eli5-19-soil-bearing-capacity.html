Soil basics and bearing capacity

Soil is solids, water and air. How much of each it holds decides its weight, and how much load it can carry before it fails under a footing.

Three phases in one block

Imagine a unit block of soil. Change the voids and the water in them and see weight, water content and density change.

What can the ground carry under a footing?

Terzaghi-type bearing capacity: cohesion, overburden and the soil’s own weight each add something. Change the soil, the footing and the water table.

Check yourself

Keep in mind

  • 1Bearing capacity comes from the soil investigation report: use its numbers, and ask for the water table range.
  • 2Settlement often governs before strength. Soft clays under big rafts settle for years.
  • 3Always check the worst water table, not the one on the day of the borehole.
  • 4Net safe pressure is what the footing can add on top of the soil already removed.
