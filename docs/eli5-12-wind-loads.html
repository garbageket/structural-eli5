Wind loads on tall buildings

Wind speeds up with height, and pressure goes with speed squared. Tall buildings catch far more than short ones, and they also sway across the wind.

Height makes the wind stronger

Pick a wind zone and terrain. Pressure climbs with height. Open terrain gives more wind than a city.

Check yourself

Keep in mind

  • 1Pressure goes with V². A 10% wind speed change is a 21% pressure change.
  • 2Across-wind response and torsion often govern tall towers, not along-wind.
  • 3Comfort (acceleration) can govern even when strength is fine.
  • 4Wind tunnel forces replace code forces. Check the load combination and scaling carefully.
