Shear walls and cores in tall buildings

Tall buildings lean into the wind. The stiff concrete core is the spine that holds them upright. Shape it right and it does the work of a whole frame.

Shape the core, change the sway

A box core is far stiffer than the same walls left open. Choose a shape and slide the sizes. The picture on the right shows the sway under wind.

Link the walls and they act as one

Two walls joined by stiff coupling beams resist as one deep section. Walls alone just add. Slide the coupling and see the stiffness grow.

Check yourself

Keep in mind

  • 1Stiffness is geometry: length³ × thickness. Spread walls wide, not thick.
  • 2Place the core near the centre of plan to limit torsion.
  • 3Openings and door cuts weaken a core a lot. Check them at the base.
  • 4Cracked stiffness: use modifiers when you check drift (see topic 26).
