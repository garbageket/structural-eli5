Bending, shear and deflection in beams

A beam does three jobs at once: it bends, it shears, it sags. See how span and depth change all three.

Load a beam and read the diagrams

Pick the support type and load. The shear and bending diagrams redraw. Then change the depth and see what it does to sag.

Depth is the cheap way to stop sagging

Deflection falls with the cube of depth. Doubling the width only halves it. Move both and compare.

Check yourself

Keep in mind

  • 1Moment goes with L², shear with L, deflection with L⁴.
  • 2Depth buys stiffness cheaply. Width is the expensive way.
  • 3A fixed-end beam has half the sag of a simply supported one, but needs top steel at the supports.
  • 4Check deflection even when strength is fine: IS 456 cl 23.2 uses span/250, and span/350 after partitions.
