Dead, live and imposed loads

Before any analysis, you have to know what the floor weighs and what people will put on it. Most design errors start with a wrong number here.

Build a floor, weigh it

Add the layers. The dead load is what the floor itself weighs. The live load comes from the use. Slide and see what dominates.

Check yourself

Keep in mind

  • 1Dead load: weigh every layer. Finishes, screed and partitions can add 2–3 kN/m².
  • 2Take partition walls as real loads, not “allowances”, when they are heavy or fixed.
  • 3Live load reduction applies to columns and foundations, not to slabs and beams.
  • 4Add the seismic share of live load to the weight for earthquake (25% below 3 kN/m², 50% above).
