Basements, deep excavation and dewatering

Dig below the water table and the ground tries to float your building. Keeping the site dry and the neighbours safe takes as much design as the structure itself.

Will the basement float?

Water pushes up on the base slab with the height of water above it. The building’s weight pushes down. See how much margin you have.

Pumping lowers the water, and the neighbours’ ground

To dig dry, wells draw the water table down. The cone of depression can stretch far in clean sand. See how much water you must pump, and how far the effect reaches.

Check yourself

Keep in mind

  • 1Design basements for the highest likely water table, and check flotation during construction too.
  • 2Waterproofing and structure work together: crack control, water stops and construction joints matter as much as thickness.
  • 3Retaining walls of a basement are propped by floors: design for K₀ plus water, not active pressure.
  • 4Monitor neighbouring buildings and the water level during dewatering.
